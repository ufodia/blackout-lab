# Community conduct

Blackout Lab welcomes people with different backgrounds and levels of technical experience.

Be considerate, specific and helpful. Discuss the behavior and evidence rather than attacking the person who reported it. Respect privacy, give credit and keep feedback focused on the project.

Harassment, discriminatory abuse, threats, doxxing, sexualized attention, repeated personal attacks and deliberate disruption are unacceptable in project spaces.

Maintainers may remove content, close discussions, limit participation or block accounts to protect the community. They should apply decisions consistently and explain them when doing so does not expose private information.

Use GitHub's reporting and blocking tools for platform abuse. For a project-conduct concern, ask for a private contact route without posting identifying details or sensitive evidence in an issue. This document does not promise a staffed or continuously monitored reporting channel.
