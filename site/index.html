A CLEAR BOUNDARY

The experiment stays in your hands.

Read the privacy notes ↗
A

Fresh browser sessions.

Captures do not copy your everyday browser login or cookies. Use pages and test environments you own or have permission to examine.

B

Local screenshots and reports.

There is no hosted audit service or cloud report upload. The test browser still contacts the target and its unblocked dependencies. Reports can contain page content and URL paths; review them before sharing.

C

Evidence with a defined scope.

Each condition captures one navigation and a bounded observation window. It does not exercise every interaction, login flow or WebSocket. A complete-looking screenshot is not a resilience certification.
